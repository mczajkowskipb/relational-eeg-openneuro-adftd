# Benchmark design

Tier 1 is the main disease benchmark: EC and EO/photo separately; tasks AD_vs_CN, AD_FTD_vs_CN, CN_vs_FTD, AD_vs_FTD; final P0/P1 feature worlds only.

Tier 2 performs paired cross-condition transfer. Tier 3 audits acquisition-condition separability. Tier 4 pools EC+EO rows with subject grouping.

Only TSP/kTSP and classic fast baselines are in current scope.
